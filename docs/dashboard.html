Top 3 Rapid Defense Questions:

Q: If payloads are encrypted, how does the model identify domains?
A: DNS response packet sizes, burst sequences, and inter-arrival times leak side-channel metadata in cleartext. Because different domains yield distinct A/AAAA record sets and CNAME chains, their volumetric flow silhouettes are unique.
Q: Why did deep sequence models (1D-CNN / GRU) achieve only 1% accuracy?
A: Encrypted DNS exchanges are compact micro-flows (8–15 packets). They lack translational sequential depth. Discriminative signal resides in aggregate volumetric envelope features (downstream bytes, burst ratios) where decision trees excel.
Q: Why does Google Public DNS show zero privacy improvement under DoH/3?
A: Google returns unpadded wire-format answers (RFC 8467). Domain-specific answer sizes pass directly into the transport stream, proving resolver implementation overrides transport protocols.
